§ 03 — The playbook for the free stuff

HOW TO ACTUALLY
use THIS ROOM.

Nobody teaches you how to consume free content without wasting your own time. Here's how I'd do it.

  1. 01

    PICK ONE — NOT ALL

    Following me on six platforms doesn't build your business. Pick the one channel that fits your actual consumption habit and go deep on it.

  2. 02

    TAKE NOTES OR DON'T BOTHER

    If you're not writing down one specific thing to execute this week, you're entertainment-watching. There's no shame in that. But don't confuse it with learning.

  3. 03

    DM ME WHEN SOMETHING HITS

    I actually read DMs on Instagram. I can't reply to all of them. But if something in a post changed what you did this week, tell me. It tells me what to make more of.

  4. 04

    DON'T BUY ANYTHING FOR 30 DAYS

    Stay in the free room for a month. If my frame doesn't fit the way you think, the paid programs won't either. Save your money. Save both of us the time.

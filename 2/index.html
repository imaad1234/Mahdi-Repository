§ 03.2 · Four weeks, no filler
Week 01 — Diagnose
Teardown.

Full audit of your business. Brand, offer, funnel, content, hires, ops. We pick the single bottleneck to break. One, not five.

Week 02 — Build
Systems.

Personal brand system. Sales SOP. First outbound motion written, scripted, staffed. You leave the week with the machine drafted.

Week 03 — Execute
Ship.

Real calls, real DMs, real outbound. Test, iterate, adjust. I'm in your WhatsApp. We break the thing together, in public, this week.

Week 04 — Install
Delegate.

Hire the role. Train the role. Hand off. You leave with a machine running, not a to-do list growing. You're now the 10%.

+ Weeks 05 – 06 reserved for refinement & follow-through. I don't leave until you ship.

What language does it run in?
English, primarily. Imaad or a member of the team can translate for Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Farsi and Mandarin if needed — mention your preferred language in the application.

Refund policy?
If after week 01 you don't believe the program will move you, tell me and I'll refund the remaining portion. No games. I also reserve the right to refund and release any applicant we realise isn't a fit.

Courses are the playbook. This is the playbook applied to you, by me, for 4–6 weeks, with the team on WhatsApp. If you want self-paced, start with the courses. If you want accountability and a sparring partner, apply here.
